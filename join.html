Friendly notice on the usage of VR headsets

Our VR headsets run on the Meta Quest 3 system, which reduces motion discomfort and offers safer engagement. Students can create VR Art in passthrough mode, where they can create while seeing the physical surroundings.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will my child experience motion sickness from using the VR?

Our VR headsets are equipped with the Meta Quest 3 system, which significantly reduces discomfort during use. Headsets are also designed to be lightweight, with improved ergonomics and accessibility.

Will my child experience claustrophobia during use?

We allow students to create VR Art in passthrough mode, which allows them to see their physical surroundings while they create.

Can my child join the programme even if they have no VR experience?

Yes, all are welcome.

What age range of students do you accept?

7 to 18 years old.
