About BENIOS

BENIOS is a Singapore based creative learning studio where art, technology, and imagination come together. Through drawing, painting, Virtual Reality (VR), and Extended Reality (XR), we empower children and communities to create, express themselves, and share meaningful stories.

Founded by internationally recognised XR artist Benedict Yu, BENIOS is among the first studios in Singapore to integrate VR and XR into visual arts education. Our team of experienced educators, artists, and facilitators creates inclusive learning experiences that nurture creativity, confidence, and curiosity.

"From hands on creativity to immersive realities, we empower Singapore's communities to create and share their stories in today's creative tech world."Benedict Yu

Our Story

For Benedict Yu, art was never just a hobby, it was a way to communicate.

Growing up with ADHD and learning difficulties, traditional classroom learning was often overwhelming. Words appeared to float across the page, making reading and writing incredibly challenging. Often seen as "the weird kid," he struggled to express himself through conventional methods.

Instead, he turned to drawing.

Art became the language that allowed him to think, communicate, and make sense of the world.

Years later, after discovering the possibilities of Virtual Reality, Benedict often reflected on one thought:

"I wish VR art existed when I was a child."

It could have given him an entirely new way to learn, create, and express ideas beyond the limitations of traditional education.

That belief became the foundation of BENIOS, a place where every child, regardless of how they learn, can discover their own creative voice through art and emerging technology.

Practical immersive-technology education, based in Singapore.
